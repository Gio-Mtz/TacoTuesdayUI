Only what you type into the waitlist form: your email, your name and, depending on whether you join as a company or as a candidate, your company name and how much you hire per year, or your main stack and your years of experience. To tell you when the platform opens and, if you asked as a company, to contact you about the roles you want to fill. We do not sell or share your email with anyone, and we do not use it to send you third-party advertising.

Until the platform opens and we have told you, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If a year after you signed up this still has not launched, we delete the list.

How you delete it

You write to {{ site.contactEmail }} from the same address you signed up with and we remove it. You do not have to explain yourself and there is no form to fill in. You can also ask us for access to what we hold about you, to correct it, or to object to our using it.

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which parts of the page work. Only if you accept. Until you accept, Google's script is not downloaded and none of its cookies are stored — it is not that it loads and does not measure: it does not load.

If you accept, Google stores its own cookies in your browser (_ga and _ga_*) to tell repeat visits apart.
